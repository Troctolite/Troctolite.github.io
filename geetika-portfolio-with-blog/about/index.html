ABOUT ME

I'm Geetika Chauhan.

I'm a hydrologist and GIS analyst based in Warsaw, Poland.

My work has moved between geology, GIS and hydrology. I began with environmental mapping and remote sensing in India, then pursued doctoral research on Polish rivers.

For my PhD research, I analysed river-flow records from 140 hydrological stations to study trends, periodicity and the influence of river engineering. I've also worked with UNESCO-IHP on international ecohydrology coordination.

I use R, QGIS and Python, and I'm interested in cartography, scientific writing and comics. This site brings together work I can share publicly and projects I create independently.
