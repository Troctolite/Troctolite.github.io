PHD RESEARCH / HYDROLOGY

How river engineering shapes flow

My doctoral research investigates how dams, weirs and other hydrotechnical structures influence river-flow regimes in Poland. I analysed long-term records from 140 hydrological stations to examine trends, periodicity and changes in annual maximum flows.
