ABOUT ME

Curious about how things work.

I'm Geetika Chauhan, a hydrologist and data analyst based in Warsaw, Poland.

My work combines hydrology, statistical analysis, GIS and data visualisation. Over the past several years, I've worked with environmental datasets before moving to hydrology.

During my doctoral research at the Institute of Geophysics, Polish Academy of Sciences, I analysed long-term river-flow records from 140 hydrological stations across Poland. My research examines how hydrotechnical structures influence river-flow regimes.

I currently work at the Institute of Inland Fisheries – National Research Institute, where I contribute to fish habitat modelling and spatial data analysis.

Before moving to Poland, I worked on GIS and remote sensing projects in India. I've also been involved in international science coordination and communication.

Outside my research work, I enjoy creating maps, exploring different ways to visualise data, and experimenting with comics and visual storytelling.

Experience & skills

My experience includes hydrological analysis, GIS, spatial data processing, statistical modelling and science communication.
