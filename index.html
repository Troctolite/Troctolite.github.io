About me

I'm Geetika Chauhan, a hydrologist and data analyst based in Warsaw, Poland.

My career journey has been like a river, flowing through different fields, from Geology to Remote Sensing and GIS, before eventually finding its way into Hydrology. I started my professional career in India and later moved to Poland to pursue a PhD in Hydrology.

The twists and turns of this journey have helped me develop a diverse set of skills, particularly in analysing environmental data and communicating findings through statistical analysis and visualisation.
