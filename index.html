02 / ABOUT ME

Scientist by training.
Curious by nature.

My work begins with questions about the natural world: how rivers respond to change, what patterns emerge from environmental data, and how we can communicate those patterns clearly.

I bring together hydrological research, GIS, data analysis and international collaboration. Outside technical work, I enjoy exploring illustration and visual storytelling as ways of making complex ideas more human.
